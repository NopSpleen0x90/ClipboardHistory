📋 ClipboardHistory

macOS Menu Bar App

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Overview

ClipboardHistory is a macOS menu bar application that monitors and displays your clipboard history locally on your device. Your privacy is our top priority.

Data Collection

ClipboardHistory does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All clipboard content is processed exclusively in memory on your local device and is never sent to any server, third-party service, or external system.

Clipboard Access

The app reads your clipboard content solely to display your clipboard history within the app interface. This data exists only in RAM for the duration of the app session and is discarded when the app is closed or the history is cleared.

Network Activity

ClipboardHistory makes no network requests of any kind. The app operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection.

Data Storage

No clipboard data is written to disk, synced to iCloud, or persisted between app launches. History is kept in memory only and is lost when the app quits.

Third-Party SDKs & Analytics

ClipboardHistory does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

Children's Privacy

ClipboardHistory does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the revised version will be published at this URL with an updated date at the top.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on GitHub.
